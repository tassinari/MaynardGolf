# 16 — Hole Map (course map, hole cameras and swipe transitions)

**Artboards:** iPhone — Hole 1 (the map at rest), Course map (the course overview, §5). There's no artboard for the transition.
**Reference:** `maynard-golf-course-v6-transitions-center.svg` in the course cartography project: the course map with one screen box per hole, an arrow for each camera move, and the extra area each swipe passes over.
**Where:** the base layer of Hole play (07-hole-play §1.1). Shot lines (14-shot-lines) draw on top of it.

## 1. Overview
The hole map isn't nine separate pictures. It's **one map of the whole course**, and each hole is a **camera** looking at part of it: a position, a direction and a zoom. Swiping between holes moves the camera, so the map **pans, rotates and zooms** from one hole to the next, turning around the **middle of the screen**. The app never loads the whole map: it ships pre-cut **hole crops** for the resting views and **transition crops** for each camera move, and keeps only the pieces next to the current hole in memory.

## 2. Course map
| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| HM-MAP-01 | One north-up **master** image of the whole course (`fullmapFull.png`, 9780 × 7586), in the app's illustrated aerial style, covering 42.440415, −71.453471 (south-west) to 42.446032, −71.443658 (north-east). The master isn't shipped; every image the app shows is cut from it (§6). Its georeference is that bounding box: latitude and longitude map linearly to pixels. |
| HM-MAP-02 | **Sharp at rest:** at rest the screen shows the hole's **hole crop**: the part of the master that hole's camera shows on the largest supported iPhone, cut from the master, rotated upright and sized to the largest phone's screen pixels (about 1320 × 2868, HEIC, about 15 MB decoded). It covers every supported screen shape at rest. |
| HM-MAP-03 | **Memory:** the map layer uses at most 150 MB while a round is open. Decoding the whole master takes about 300 MB (596 MB peak, measured in #128), so it's never loaded. Instead `CourseImageryStore` keeps only the pieces next to the current round hole: its crop, the crops of the round holes before and after it, and the two transitions to them. A round doesn't wrap, so hole 1 and the last hole have one neighbour; in an 18-hole round 9 → 10 is the 9 → 1 transition. The window ends with the course overview (about 10 MB, HM-OV-01), loaded after the hole pieces. That's about 125 MB at most. Moving on releases the pieces that are no longer adjacent and loads the new neighbours in the background, one at a time and the current hole's crop first: a decode can't be stopped once started, so at most one runs and fast swipes never stack decodes past the budget. A piece that fails isn't retried (the files are bundled); the failure is logged. |
| HM-MAP-07 | **Transition crops.** Each camera move is fixed, so each transition's image is cut ahead of time: the map-aligned area every frame of that swipe shows on any supported iPhone, at **half the master's resolution** (21–46 MB decoded; 9 → 1 is the largest). It's in motion whenever it's on screen. Half resolution is the starting point; a third (9–21 MB) is the fallback if memory needs it, judged on a device by holding a swipe partway. |
| HM-MAP-08 | **Seamless layering.** During a swipe the transition crop draws underneath, and the hole crops you're leaving and going to draw on top, all through the **same camera**. The sharp crop stays wherever it still covers the screen, and the transition crop shows only in the areas it uncovers, so nothing visibly switches when a swipe starts or ends. Every piece comes from the same master pixels, so they always match. A render test checks that each hole crop lines up with its transition crops at progress 0 and 1. |
| HM-MAP-04 | **No dead space:** the images cover every point the screen can show, at rest and during every transition (1→2 … 8→9, and 9→1 for the second nine). A unit test proves it (HM-TS-03). The master has no holes inside it. **Not met yet:** some views go past the master's edge (iPhone 16 / Pro Max): 7→8 6.4 % of the screen, 1→2 3.3 %, 2→3 2.5 %, hole 9 at rest and 8→9 / 9→1 1.0 %, 4→5 0.9 %, 3→4 touches the edge; on iPhone SE about 40 % less. The master needs extending about 600 px left (y 1300–5100, beside hole 1), 520 px top (x 1100–4600, above holes 1–2), 730 px bottom (x 3000–8800, under holes 9 to 8) and 250 px right (y 4500–7000, beside holes 7–8), in master pixels. Until then those areas are blank, and the crops pick up the new area when they're re-cut. |
| HM-MAP-05 | Dark mode dims the map as in DS-MT-05. There's one set of artwork, not a second dark version. |
| HM-MAP-06 | Accessibility label per hole stays "Aerial view of hole {n}, par {p}" (HP-MAP-01). |

## 3. Hole cameras
| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| HM-CAM-01 | Each hole has a camera in `CourseImagery.json` (HM-CD-01), from the stitch registration: the hole frame's `mapCenter` on the master (master pixels), its `rotationDegrees`, and `mapPixelsPerHolePixel`. The hole frame (2048 × 4096) is shown aspect-fill, centred, so the hole's centre is the middle of the screen (`ScreenCamera(hole:holeImage:screenSize:)`). GPS reaches the map through the master's georeference (HM-MAP-01). |
| HM-CAM-02 | At rest, the tee is near the bottom of the screen and the green near the top, and both stay clear of the top bar, hole card, map buttons and leaderboard (SL-DR-07). On every supported iPhone, every tee box, approach shot and the pin is at least 24 pt from the sides, at least 110 pt below the top and 150 pt above the bottom (74 / 116 pt on a screen without a home indicator), and the pin is at least 150 pt from the right edge (the hole card); none of them is under the Out of order button's corner, 86 pt from the left edge and up to 86 pt above that bottom limit (HP-OO-08); the view stays on the master. `tools/frame-hole-cameras.py` frames the cameras to these limits (#186). |
| HM-CAM-03 | The same camera data works on every iPhone size: `width` is relative to the screen width, and the visible ground is taller on taller screens. |
| HM-CAM-04 | Holes 10–18 use the cameras of holes 1–9 (CRS-HC-01). |
| HM-CAM-05 | A pure `ScreenCamera` (centre on the master, screen points per master pixel, rotation) turns a camera into a transform from master pixels to screen points, turning around the middle of the screen, and `ScreenCamera.blend` moves between two (HM-TR-01). Shot lines, the pin and "my location" will use the same transform through the georeference (14-shot-lines, #108). |

## 4. Swipe transition
| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| HM-TR-01 | Swiping between holes (HP-NAV-01) drives the camera **interactively**: the map follows the finger. Progress 0 → 1 blends the camera's `center` along the straight line between the two holes' centers, the heading the **short way round**, and the width. The map **turns around the middle of the screen**; the tee isn't pinned. |
| HM-TR-02 | Letting go past halfway (or with a flick) finishes the move; otherwise it springs back. The finish takes about 0.5 s with ease-out and never overshoots the rotation. A drag that starts on a target cross moves the target, not the hole (14-shot-lines SL-TG-03). |
| HM-TR-03 | Auto-advance (HP-NAV-02) uses a camera move of about 0.9 s (`MapSwipe.flyDuration`). The Course map's zoom out and back in (§5) takes 0.6 s (`MapSwipe.overviewDuration`). A move to a hole that isn't a neighbour (the Out of order button, or auto-advance past complete holes, HP-OO-04, 06) flies out to the overview and in to that hole, 0.35 s each way (`MapSwipe.jumpLegDuration`), so a jump back takes under a second (#227). |
| HM-TR-04 | The hole card crossfades to the next hole at the halfway point (HP-HOLE-03). The top bar, map buttons and leaderboard don't move. When shot lines arrive (#108), the shot line, targets, yardage panel and pin fade out when a transition starts and draw on when the camera settles (SL-MO-01). |
| HM-TR-05 | For turns of more than 120°, the camera also zooms out by up to 20 % at the midpoint, so the turn reads as flying over the course. This covers hole 4 → 5, which is nearly a U-turn (about 170°); no pair needs a crossfade. |
| HM-TR-06 | **Reduce Motion:** no pan or rotation. The current map crossfades to the next hole's resting view. |
| HM-TR-07 | Smooth on the oldest supported iPhone: no hitches over 16 ms while dragging (checked in Instruments). Nothing is decoded during a swipe: the pieces both ways are loaded before the finger moves (`BundleCourseImageryLoader` decodes off the main actor, in full). If a move's pieces haven't finished loading, the drag doesn't move the camera (a drag that turns back toward such a side straightens it), and letting go crossfades to the next hole instead of showing a blank. |
| HM-TR-08 | In an 18-hole round, hole 9 → 10 moves from camera 9 to camera 1. A round doesn't wrap: a swipe never goes before hole 1 or after the last hole; past either end it only stretches (HM-TR-09). |
| HM-TR-09 | **Rubber band at the ends (#181):** dragging back on hole 1, or forward on the last hole (9 or 18), leans the camera toward the hole across the end (the last hole, or hole 1) with growing resistance (`MapSwipe.rubberBand`: approaching 12 % of that camera move however far the finger goes, 80 % of it at one screen width) and springs back on release (about 0.4 s, with a small bounce that overshoots slightly past rest); the hole never changes. It draws the course overview under the hole's crop, so no transition crop is loaded for it (HM-MAP-03). A light `.selection` haptic plays when the stretch starts. The shot line, targets and panel fade as in any move (HM-TR-04); the hole card, top bar, map buttons and leaderboard don't move. Off with Reduce Motion (HM-TR-06), while the overview is open, and before the overview image has loaded: the drag then does nothing. The Course map and the Out of order button don't start a move while a stretch is live. |

## 5. Course overview (Course map button)
From the **Course map** artboard (#227). This fills in HP-CTL-02.

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| HM-OV-01 | The Course map button zooms the camera out from the current hole to the whole course in 0.6 s (HM-TR-03): it pans, turns the short way and zooms. The overview is **turned to fit a portrait screen**: the manifest's `overviewRotationDegrees` (−105°, from the artboard: the clubhouse at the bottom, the course running up the screen; written by `tools/cut-course-map.py --overview-rotation`, HM-CD-02), north up when a manifest has none. The overview is **cropped to the course and fills the screen** (`CourseOverview.camera`, #227): it zooms so the box around every round hole's tee (for the round's tee) and green covers the screen, the tighter side touching its edges, so a tee can be cut off. It zooms out only as far as it must to keep every badge inside the clear area (130 pt below the top, 50 pt above the bottom, 48 pt from the sides, `CourseOverview.ClearInsets`), centred there. The master always covers the screen: the zoom never goes out past that point and the camera slides back onto the master, so none of the map's ground shows. When the two can't both be met, the map wins over a badge's clear area (on the smallest iPhone a badge can sit a point or two past it). The whole course is drawn from **`overview.heic`**, the master at a sixth of its resolution (1953 × 1530, about 12 MB decoded, HM-CD-02), with the current hole's sharp crop on top where it still covers the screen. The crop is brighter than the overview image, so it fades out over the first 35 % of the zoom (`MapSwipe.overviewCropFade`) and back in over the last 35 % of the way in; its outline never shows over the course. A numbered `HoleNumberBadge` with a 2.5 pt Chalk border (in a 44 pt tap target), upright however the map is turned, sits in each hole's fairway once the zoom ends (`CourseOverview.badgePoint`): on the first approach shot (SL-CD-02; on a par 5, the first of its two), or on the green's centre on a par 3. In an 18-hole round 10–18 sit beside 1–9, which share their course holes. The overview loads last in the map's window (HM-MAP-03), and the button is disabled until it has loaded. |
| HM-OV-02 | Tapping a badge makes that hole the current one and flies the camera from the overview to it, as if swiped there. **Exit** flies back to the hole it was opened from. Tapping the map does nothing (no pinch or pan, HP-MAP-06). Swipes are off while the overview is open. With Reduce Motion, opening and closing crossfade (HM-TR-06). |
| HM-OV-03 | The current hole's badge has a Chalk ring. Holes where every player has a score show a check. VoiceOver reads "Hole 5", adding ", current hole" and ", scored". |
| HM-OV-04 | **Only the map and two controls** while the overview is open from the Course map button (`isBrowsingOverview`): **Exit** (a glass pill with a back chevron, top leading; VoiceOver "Back to hole {n}") and **Show my location** (a 44 pt glass circle, top trailing). The hole card, the Exit / Done of the round, the Card, Course map and Show my location row, the leaderboard and score board, the Out of order button and the shot lines are removed (not just transparent) while it's open, so no scores are entered from the overview. They fade out as the zoom starts and back in as the camera flies back to a hole, the hole card and Out of order with them (`isLeavingOverview`); they take no taps until the camera lands, so no score is saved during the flight. Auto-advance (HP-NAV-02) doesn't move the hole under the overview. A flight through the overview to a far hole (the Out of order button, a far auto-advance, HP-OO-04, 06) isn't browsing: Hole play keeps its controls and no badges show. Badges appear once the camera has arrived and hide as soon as it leaves. If a picked hole's sharp crop is still decoding when the camera arrives, the map shows that hole through the overview image until the crop is ready, never a blank map. |
| HM-OV-05 | **Show my location** on the overview is the same toggle as Hole play's (HP-CTL-03): filled while on, the same permission, alerts and fix rules. While on with a fix on the course, the blue `LocationDot` sits at the player's position on the overview (`HolePlayViewModel.overviewLocation`, through the georeference, HM-MAP-01), shown with the badges. Turning it on or off here carries back to the hole. |

## 6. Course data
| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| HM-CD-01 | `CourseImagery.json` (bundled, generated) lists the master's pixel size and georeference and, per hole, the camera (**center on the master in master pixels, rotation and master pixels per hole-image pixel**, from the stitch registration `course-stitch-v1/stitch.py`, ×2 for the full master), plus each hole crop and transition crop, and the `overview`, with its file name and **placement on the master** (origin, scale and rotation), so the app positions every piece with the same camera math. GPS (HM-MAP-01) is reached through the georeference. |
| HM-CD-02 | **Cutting script** (`tools/cut-course-map.py`, in the repo): reads the master and the cameras and writes `hole1…9.heic`, `transition-1-2…9-1.heic`, `overview.heic` (the whole master north up, `--overview-divisor`, default 6) and `CourseImagery.json`. Every run also writes `overviewRotationDegrees`, the Course map's turn (`--overview-rotation`, default −105, HM-OV-01). `--overview-only` writes just the overview, its entry and the rotation in the existing `CourseImagery.json`, leaving the other files untouched. It never changes the master. When the master or the stitch changes, the script is re-run; nothing is tuned by hand. Areas past the master's edge are left blank (transparent). |
| HM-CD-03 | Per-hole aerial images and their three control points (14-shot-lines SL-CD-03) are no longer needed: the hole crops are cut from the master. About 25–35 MB of crops ship in the app (to be measured when the script exists), in place of one 7.6 MB map. |
